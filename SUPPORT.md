# Fennavio support

Fennavio is an independent experimental fork for personal use. No public app
release or public support service is offered. The maintainer handles local
setup and development through the fork's review discussions. Do not direct
Fennavio installation, signing or adaptation problems to Crest's maintainer,
community, roadmap or download channels.

Before accepting external users, establish and publish the fork's actual
support channel. Until then, do not imply that a disabled issue tracker or an
unconfigured contact route is an available support service.

Report suspected security issues confidentially; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not post browsing history, account information, passwords, tokens or private
URLs in public discussions.

The upstream [Crest project](https://github.com/pauljoda/Crest) has its own
support policy. Escalate a reproducible upstream defect there only after
checking it against upstream and following its reporting rules.
